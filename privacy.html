LingoLines Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

LingoLines is a Chrome extension that shows YouTube subtitles in two languages at once.

What LingoLines collects

Nothing. LingoLines does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information, browsing history, or usage data. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no servers of its own.

What stays on your device

Your settings (second language, text size, line order, and on/off state) are saved with Chrome's built-in storage. If you use Chrome Sync, Chrome syncs them to your other computers the same way it syncs your other extension settings. LingoLines never sees or receives them.

LingoLines also keeps two numbers on your computer only: the date you first used it and how many videos you've watched with it (up to five). It uses them to decide when to ask, once, whether you'd like to rate it. It never records which videos you watch.

How it works

LingoLines runs only on youtube.com. To show subtitles, it requests the video's caption tracks, and YouTube's own translations of them, directly from YouTube, the same way YouTube's player does. Those requests go only to YouTube and are covered by Google's Privacy Policy. The subtitles are shown on the page and are not saved or sent anywhere else.

Changes

If LingoLines ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the change will be announced in the extension before it takes effect.
